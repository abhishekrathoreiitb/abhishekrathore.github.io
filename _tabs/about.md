# About Me

I’m Abhishek, a technology professional who enjoys exploring the space where **technology, engineering, data, and real-world problems** meet.

My work has given me the opportunity to spend time around financial technology, payment systems, APIs, enterprise platforms, and large-scale technology ecosystems. Over time, my curiosity has expanded beyond simply building or operating systems—I’ve become increasingly interested in understanding **why systems behave the way they do, how they can be made smarter, and what happens when we experiment with new approaches**.

I enjoy learning by building. Rather than following a purely theoretical path, I prefer taking an idea, creating a small experiment, looking at what happens, and then iterating. This has led me to explore areas such as **machine learning, graph-based approaches, fraud detection, LLMs, AI engineering, and developer tools**.

This blog is an extension of that journey.

I’ll use it to document experiments, technical explorations, things I learn, things that don’t work, and occasionally the rabbit holes I inevitably fall into. Some posts may be polished conclusions; others may simply capture a thought or experiment that eventually leads somewhere unexpected.

My goal isn't to pretend I have all the answers. It is to **learn in public, build things, question assumptions, and share the journey**.

If you enjoy technology, experimentation, systems thinking, AI, or simply exploring “what if?” questions, you’ll probably find something interesting here.

**Code. Experiment. Learn. Repeat.**
